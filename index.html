What We Fix in Cedar Hill

From the most common trip-over-the-cable HDMI breaks to complex board diagnostics.

HDMI Port Replacement

Bent pins, loose connection, black screen, or TV displaying "No Signal" while console power light is white. We desolder the broken port and install an upgraded, reinforced OEM connector.

Supported: PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One

Overheating & Thermal Service

Loud fan roaring like a jet engine, or console shutting down without warning during gameplay. We clean heatsinks, replace thermal paste, and resurface oxidized liquid metal on PS5 APUs.

Supported: PS5, PS4 Pro, Xbox Series X, Switch
Request Thermal Service Quote →

No Power / Board Diagnostics

Console beeps once and shuts off, has no lights, or was hit by a power surge or thunderstorm. We isolate board-level shorts, blown power supply rails, and faulty capacitors.

Supported: All PlayStation, Xbox & Nintendo Consoles
Request Diagnostic Quote →

Switch USB-C & Power ICs

Nintendo Switch won't charge, won't dock, or draws 0.00 amps. We replace bent 24-pin dual-row USB-C ports, damaged M92T36 power chips, and BQ24193 charging controllers.

Supported: Switch OLED, Standard V1/V2, Switch Lite

Disc Drive & Laser Repair

Console won't take discs, makes clicking or grinding noises, or gives "Unrecognized Disc" errors. We align mechanical rollers, clear jams, or replace worn optical laser pickups.

Controller Repair & Drift Fix

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Gamers Ask Us

Everything you need to know before dropping off your PlayStation, Xbox, or Switch.

How long does a console HDMI port repair typically take?
Same-day service is available on common HDMI repairs (such as PS5 and Xbox Series X) when parts are in stock and the repair workload allows. Most standard HDMI port replacements take between 1 to 3 hours from drop-off to testing completion.
Will my game save files, digital downloads, and accounts be wiped?
No. We specialize in physical hardware and board-level repairs (HDMI ports, power chips, thermal cooling, power supply). Your internal SSD/HDD storage is preserved, meaning your game installs, profiles, and save files remain completely intact.
Do I need to make an appointment or can I just walk in?
Walk-ins are always welcome during our open hours (Monday–Saturday, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM). However, calling ahead at 817-449-4211 can help us confirm component parts on hand and give you the most accurate turnaround window for that day.
What kind of warranty comes with your console repairs?
We offer warranty protection on qualifying repairs. Our replacement parts are OEM-grade and thoroughly stress tested under 4K gaming loads before you pick up. Ask your technician for specific warranty terms on your repair category.
What if my console is determined to not be repairable?
We believe in honest, transparent diagnostics. If your console has catastrophic APU silicon damage or severe liquid corrosion that makes repair uneconomical, we will explain the diagnosis clearly so you never pay for unnecessary work.
